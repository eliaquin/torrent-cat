# Contribution policy

TorrentCat is an open-source project maintained by Eliaquín Encarnación. It is not accepting external contributions, pull requests, patches, translations, or offers to join as a maintainer. Pull requests are disabled on this repository.

You are welcome to use, study, modify, redistribute, and maintain your own fork under the terms of the [MIT license](LICENSE), including its notice requirements. This contribution policy does not change those license permissions.

## Bug reports

GitHub issues are available for reproducible bug reports. Please include your macOS version, Mac architecture, the commit or version you built, reproduction steps, and expected versus actual behavior. Remove personal file paths, private tracker URLs, credentials, and other sensitive information from logs before sharing them.

Please do not submit patches through issues to bypass the contribution policy. Reports may inform the maintainer's work, but responses, fixes, feature requests, and support are not guaranteed.
